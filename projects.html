Anthropomorphic Prosthetic Hand for Precision and Tool-Oriented Grasping

2023 – Present · Prosthetic Hand · Embedded Actuation · Precision Pinch · Diagonal Power Grip

This project develops an anthropomorphic prosthetic hand that supports diagonal power grip, tip pinch, and lateral pinch within a compact embedded-actuation platform. The design combines active palm articulation, passive thumb–finger actuation-order switching, differential tendon routing, and dual-motor tendon actuation modules.

Key Design Ideas

  • Optimized the active palm-joint locations to improve tool-oriented diagonal power grip stability.
  • Designed a spring–magnet threshold mechanism that passively switches thumb–finger actuation order for tip pinch and lateral pinch.
  • Integrated differential tendon routing and dual-motor tendon actuation modules into a compact prosthetic hand prototype.

My Contributions

  • Designed the prosthetic hand mechanism, active palm structure, and overall hand architecture.
  • Developed the geometric palm-joint optimization method and passive actuation-order mechanism.
  • Built and tested the integrated prototype through pull-out tests, motion capture, fingertip force measurements, and functional demonstrations.

Technical Skills

Prosthetic Hand Design Tendon-driven Mechanism Underactuated Mechanism Differential Actuation Embedded Actuation SOLIDWORKS MATLAB Motion Capture Force Measurement

Results

  • Demonstrated diagonal power grip, tip pinch, and lateral pinch in one integrated prosthetic hand.
  • Validated the active palm design through geometric modeling and pull-out experiments.
  • Demonstrated representative tasks including hammering, holding a frying pan, transferring a nail, and holding a card.

Posture-Dependent Variable Transmission Mechanism for Prosthetic Hands

2022 – 2024 · Variable Transmission · Tendon-driven Prosthetic Finger · ISR 2024

This project developed a posture-dependent variable transmission mechanism for tendon-driven prosthetic fingers. The mechanism uses a spiral-shaped spool and a series elastic compression spring to enable fast pre-contact finger motion and stronger post-contact tendon force.

Key Design Ideas

  • Designed a spiral spool whose effective radius decreases as the tendon winds, changing the speed–force ratio according to finger posture.
  • Integrated a series elastic compression spring to allow additional tendon winding after object contact.
  • Modeled and experimentally evaluated the effects of spool geometry and spring characteristics on grasping speed and fingertip force.

My Contributions

  • Proposed and developed the PDVT concept for tendon-driven prosthetic fingers.
  • Designed the spiral spool, spring mechanism, prosthetic finger, and experimental setup.
  • Conducted parametric studies and performance tests for grasping speed, fingertip force, and durability.

Technical Skills

Variable Transmission Tendon-driven Mechanism Spiral Spool Series Elastic Spring Mechanical Modeling 3D Printing Motor Encoder Load Cell

Results

  • Achieved up to 3.7× faster grasping speed compared with a conventional rigid small-spool mechanism.
  • Achieved up to 5× stronger grip force compared with a conventional rigid large-spool mechanism.
  • Fabricated a 70 g PDVT-equipped prosthetic finger and validated posture-dependent speed–force behavior.

Publication: Posture-dependent variable transmission mechanism for prosthetic hand inspired by human grasping characteristics

High-Power Prosthetic Hand with Enhanced Durability

2020 – 2025 · Prosthetic Hand · High-Power Actuation · Durability Test

This project focused on developing a human-hand-sized prosthetic hand system capable of fast grasping, high fingertip force, and long-term tendon-driven durability. The design integrated high-power motors, tendon routing, moving-pulley differential actuation, FEM analysis, and repeated actuation testing.

Key Design Ideas

  • Designed a prosthetic hand targeting grasping within 1 second and fingertip force greater than 45 N.
  • Developed tendon routing and a moving-pulley differential mechanism for compact high-power actuation.
  • Designed and evaluated high-load components using FEM simulations and durability experiments.

My Contributions

  • Proposed the original hardware concept and designed the tendon-driven actuator structure.
  • Designed the moving-pulley differential mechanism, tendon routing, and tension-maintaining mechanism.
  • Built prototypes and conducted repeated actuation experiments for long-term durability validation.

Technical Skills

Prosthetic Hand Design High-Power Actuation Moving-Pulley Differential Tendon Routing SOLIDWORKS SOLIDWORKS FEM Durability Test Force Measurement

Results

  • Designed a human-hand-sized prosthetic hand targeting fast grasping and fingertip force greater than 45 N.
  • Validated high-load moving-pulley components through FEM simulation.
  • Demonstrated tendon-driven operation over more than 300,000 repeated actuation cycles.

Anthropomorphic Prosthetic Hand for Sports Swing Activities

2017 – 2021 · Prosthetic Hand · Articulated Palm · Tendon Routing · TMECH

This project developed an anthropomorphic prosthetic hand for stable diagonal power squeeze grip in sports swing activities. The hand mechanism was inspired by human palm concavity, CMC/MCP joint arthrokinematics, and FDP/FDS tendon routing.

Key Design Ideas

  • Designed articulated fourth and fifth CMC joints to reproduce palm concavity through flexion and supination.
  • Designed 2-DOF MCP joints and FDP/FDS-inspired tendon routing for adaptive diagonal grasping.
  • Implemented differential tendon-driven actuation to drive multiple finger tendons with fewer actuators.

My Contributions

  • Designed the hand mechanism, articulated palm joints, MCP joints, and tendon routing.
  • Implemented differential tendon-driven actuation and integrated motor units and electronics.
  • Conducted pressure distribution and impact-resistance experiments to evaluate grasp performance.

Technical Skills

Anthropomorphic Hand Design Articulated Palm CMC Joint Design MCP Joint Design FDP/FDS Tendon Routing Differential Actuation Pressure Measurement Impact Test

Results

  • Demonstrated stable diagonal power squeeze grip using an articulated prosthetic hand mechanism.
  • Increased ulnar-side finger participation compared with a non-articulated palm design.
  • Improved grasp resilience against external impact through articulated palm and tendon-driven finger adaptation.

Publication: Anthropomorphic Prosthetic Hand Inspired by Efficient Swing Mechanics for Sports Activities

Loco-sheet: Morphing Inchworm Robot Across Rough Terrain

2017 – 2019 · Soft Robotics · Morphing Locomotion · RoboSoft Competition

Loco-sheet is a flexible sheet-based soft locomotion robot developed to traverse rough and constrained environments. The robot uses a tendon-driven PVC sheet body that morphs between S-shaped and omega-shaped locomotion modes.

Key Design Ideas

  • Used the bending stiffness of a flexible sheet as a functional locomotion element for climbing and crawling.
  • Implemented S-shaped locomotion for stair climbing and omega-shaped locomotion for low-clearance traversal.
  • Integrated anisotropic friction wheels and tendon-driven morphing for untethered locomotion.

My Contributions

  • Proposed the main hardware concept of the flexible sheet-based locomotion robot body.
  • Designed and fabricated the robot hardware, tendon-driven actuation mechanism, and PCB.
  • Implemented Arduino-based motor position control, wireless control, and system integration for RoboSoft competition.

Technical Skills

Soft Robotics Morphing Mechanism Tendon-driven Actuation SOLIDWORKS Arduino Motor Position Control PCB Design Laser Cutting

Results

  • Demonstrated fully untethered locomotion using onboard motors, electronics, and battery.
  • Climbed 90 mm stairs and passed through 72 mm low-clearance gaps.
  • Achieved 2nd place in RoboSoft 2018 and 1st place in RoboSoft 2019 locomotion competitions.

Publication: Loco-sheet: Morphing Inchworm Robot Across Rough-terrain
