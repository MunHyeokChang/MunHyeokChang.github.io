Selected Projects

Loco-sheet: Flexible Sheet-based Locomotion Robot for Various Uneven Environments

2017–2019 · Soft Robotics · Locomotion Robot · RoboSoft Competition

Loco-sheet is a flexible sheet-based soft locomotion robot developed for the IEEE RoboSoft Locomotion Competition. The robot was designed to traverse various uneven environments, including sand, turning paths, stairs, and low-clearance spaces, while satisfying size constraints.

Project Overview

The robot used the stiffness change generated by folding and unfolding an elastic flexible sheet. By combining this morphing sheet structure with tendon-driven actuation, the robot could switch between multiple locomotion modes for different environments.

Key Ideas

  • S-shaped configuration: The robot folded into an S-shape to raise its center of mass and overcome obstacles such as stairs.
  • Omega-shaped configuration: The robot formed an omega-like body shape and used anisotropic wheels to crawl through low-clearance spaces.
  • Turning motion: The robot generated turning motion by selectively folding and unfolding one side of the sheet while maintaining an S-shaped configuration.

My Contributions

  • Proposed the main hardware concept of the flexible sheet-based locomotion robot body.
  • Designed and fabricated the robot hardware.
  • Implemented the tendon-driven actuation mechanism for sheet folding and unfolding.
  • Designed and fabricated the PCB for the robot system.
  • Implemented Arduino-based motor position control and wireless control.
